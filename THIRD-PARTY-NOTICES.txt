bunaway framework artifact

The artifact includes native build source/tools and pinned dependency manifests,
not precompiled Bun or WebView2 binaries. Windows preparation tools download pinned archives/files and check their SHA-256
hashes. macOS builds verify the pinned Bun executable and use direct FFI to
system frameworks, without project-owned C/ObjC++ binaries.

License texts included in licenses/ (verified against the manifests):
- LICENSE.bun: Bun 1.4.2, source revision and license URL in
  runtime/build-manifests/{windows-x64,darwin-aarch64,android-x64,android-arm64}.json. This upstream text
  includes Bun's third-party attributions; preserve it in full.
- License-WebView2.txt: Microsoft WebView2 SDK 1.0.4129.50; archive URL/hash and
  license hash in native/windows/bun/deps.json.

Generated apps retain these notices. Final app builds include the runtime/native
dependency license texts through the existing native build pipeline. App authors
are responsible for their own code and additional dependencies' licenses.

iOS uses the pinned Bun XCFramework archive in runtime/build-manifests/ios-arm64.json.
Generated iOS apps preserve its LICENSE.md and LICENSE-libffi in full, together
with FRAMEWORK-LICENSE.txt and this notice, in bunaway-assets/licenses/.
